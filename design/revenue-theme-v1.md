## Conversion rules
- One primary CTA per screen, e.g. 'Book an assessment'.
- Show NZD pricing, scope, privacy policy and contact method clearly.
- Never use green to imply unverified scans or claims.
- Meet WCAG contrast targets: 4.5:1 normal text, 3:1 large text and controls.
- Track page view -> CTA click -> enquiry -> booked -> paid, with privacy-conscious analytics.
- Run an A/B test before claiming that colours increase conversion.

## Distribution shortlist
